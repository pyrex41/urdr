- Order, locale-like collation, and exception-prose leak detection

## Explicit non-claims

This milestone makes **no D0, D1, or D2 whole-system claim**. M0 does not
establish controlled guest boundaries, Firecracker device/time/entropy policy,
observable full-system replay, network broker determinism, or certification.
Those remain later hard gates.
