## What is claimed

- Modeled abstract world: component interface, scenario DSL, NetKAT
  fragment, NetworkPolicy compiler (`spec-semantics-only`), properties,
  event log / replay / modeled-run certificates, seeded exploration and
  shrinking.
- Wave I reference scenario `scenarios/abstract/partition-retry.shen`
  validates under the scenario DSL (three processes, partition fault,
  liveness deadline).
- Seven-item exit demonstration (`shen/tests/integration/`) on **shen-cl**
  (owner directive: single-port development from Wave E; see
  `docs/plans/m1-agent-team.md` §5). Earlier Wave I work also recorded
  shen-go exact-golden; digests match the checked-in goldens.
  1. `explore` discovers the injected partition failure from a seed
  2. `shrink` removes at least one irrelevant choice; failure persists
  3. `replay` of the minimized artifact reproduces the failure/verdicts
  4. NetKAT isolation: partition policy yields symbolic `unreachable`;
     open-fabric reachability witness is `confirmed` (not `unconfirmed`)
  5. NetworkPolicy: default-deny-all + multi-peer (`test-network-policy`)
     compiles with isolation claim; over-permissive allow-all-ingress
     admits an unintended flow (concrete admission witness)
  6. Modeled-run certificate: zero non-`pure`/`modeled` events
     (`abstract=true`), markers `modeled-world-only` and
     `spec-semantics-only`
  7. Strict Bifrost gate on **shen-cl**:
     `make conformance REQUIRED_IMPLS=shen-cl` (four-port deferred)

## What is not claimed

- No D0–D3 whole-system profile.
- Certificates are **modeled-world-only**; they do not certify guest,
  Firecracker, UAP-transport, or live CNI paths.
- NetworkPolicy results are **spec-semantics-only** (ADR 0004 Decision 4);
  no CNI fidelity claim (M4).
- Prolog is **not certifying** (Wave G spike / fallback; not required for
  M1 exit).
- Cross-runtime byte-identity for Waves E–I suites awaits the four-port
  sweep (agent 23 / separate port-perf work). Waves A–D / C2 retain their
  prior four-port evidence where recorded at merge.
- Four-port byte-identity for Waves E–I remains open.
